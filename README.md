# Olymp Ex Brand Launch

Build a state-of-the-art, premium corporate website and complete digital brand experience for my Egyptian agritrade export company, Olymp Ex. This is a serious B2B international trading/export company specializing in fresh and frozen fruits and vegetables from Egypt. Create the full brand direction end-to-end inside the website: logo treatment/wordmark concept for “Olymp Ex”, brand identity, color system, typography, iconography, photography direction, UI components, voice, and responsive web design.

CORE BRAND: Olymp Ex. Position it as a trusted, modern Egyptian gateway for premium agricultural products to global markets. Visual identity must use a predominantly white background with agricultural green and sophisticated purple as the primary brand colors. Make it feel premium, contemporary, precise, global, fresh, and trustworthy—not like a generic farm website. Use restrained gradients, subtle glass/blur where appropriate, elegant borders, generous whitespace, sophisticated editorial composition, and polished micro-interactions. Avoid excessive rounded cards, cliché leaves, rustic farm aesthetics, stock-photo overload, dark full-page layouts, and visual clutter.

BRAND COLORS: Primary agricultural green around #2F7D32; deep green #174B2A; sophisticated purple around #6B3FA0; deep purple #45206B; white #FFFFFF; very light neutral backgrounds. Establish semantic tokens and accessible contrast. Green should communicate agriculture, freshness and provenance; purple should communicate sophistication, global trade and differentiation.

TYPOGRAPHY: Use a premium modern sans-serif such as Manrope/Inter for UI and a complementary elegant display treatment if useful. Strong typographic hierarchy, tight headlines, excellent readability.

SITE ARCHITECTURE: Home, About Olymp Ex, Products, Fresh Produce, Frozen Produce, Quality & Traceability, Global Markets / Export, Sustainability, Contact / Request a Quote. Include a dedicated product detail pattern and a strong quote/contact conversion flow.

HOMEPAGE: Create an exceptional hero with the headline “Egypt’s Harvest. Delivered to the World.” and supporting message about sourcing, quality control, cold-chain capability, and reliable international delivery. Primary CTA “Request a Quote”; secondary CTA “Explore Products”. Use a high-end agricultural/product image composition with fresh Egyptian produce, subtle map/globe/export cues, and refined green-purple graphic accents. Add a trust strip, featured product categories, “From Egypt to the World” export story, quality/traceability process, fresh vs frozen capabilities, market coverage, sustainability section, testimonials/partner trust area, and final CTA.

PRODUCTS: Showcase premium Egyptian categories such as strawberries, grapes, citrus/oranges, pomegranates, onions, potatoes, green beans, peas, mixed vegetables and other seasonally available produce. Do not fabricate certifications, exact countries, facilities, volumes, or claims; label examples as representative where needed. Include filters/categories and product cards with origin, format (fresh/frozen), seasonality, packaging, and availability placeholders. Make the UI easy to extend with real data later.

QUALITY & TRACEABILITY: Visually explain a rigorous process: sourcing → inspection → grading → packing → cold chain → documentation → shipment → destination. Use elegant timeline/process UI. Mention food safety and quality as principles without inventing certifications.

GLOBAL MARKETS: Use a tasteful world/Egypt export visualization, with Egypt as the source and destination regions represented abstractly. Avoid claiming actual current market coverage unless presented as editable placeholders.

ABOUT: Tell a premium brand story centered on Egypt’s agricultural heritage, modern export discipline, dependable partnerships, and international ambition. Make the company feel like a sophisticated exporter/trading partner, not a local farm.

CONTACT / RFQ: High-conversion form with product/category, fresh/frozen, estimated volume, destination market, packaging, target shipment date, company/contact details, message. Include WhatsApp/email/phone placeholders as editable content rather than inventing details.

NAVIGATION: Premium sticky header, logo on left, concise nav, language selector (EN / AR) as UI-ready, CTA on right. Include mobile navigation. Build accessible semantic HTML, keyboard-friendly controls, responsive behavior from mobile to ultrawide desktop, SEO-ready page titles/meta structure, OpenGraph placeholders, and polished loading/hover/focus states.

DESIGN DETAILS: Create a real design system in the code: CSS variables/tokens for colors, spacing, radii, shadows, typography; reusable buttons, cards, badges, section headers, product tiles, stats, forms, nav, footer, modal/drawer patterns. Use subtle agricultural-inspired line motifs and geometric export/grid motifs rather than literal leaf illustrations everywhere. Purple is an accent, not a competing background. Use real-looking high-quality produce imagery where available, but structure the app so image assets can be replaced easily. Add tasteful motion: reveal-on-scroll, image parallax/scale, hover elevation, navigation transitions, but respect prefers-reduced-motion.

FOOTER: Olymp Ex wordmark, short positioning statement, navigation, product categories, contact placeholders, social placeholders, legal links, and “Egypt” origin statement.

IMPORTANT: This must look like a top-tier international B2B agritrade/export company in 2026, suitable for buyers, importers, distributors, wholesalers and food-service partners. Do not make it look like a template. Prioritize visual hierarchy, conversion, credibility, product presentation, and brand consistency. Build the actual working responsive site, not just a design description. Use TypeScript + Tailwind/shadcn conventions of Lovable and keep content/components maintainable.
